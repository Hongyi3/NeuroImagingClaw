# Benchmark Acceptance Criteria

A dataset may enter the official benchmark suite only if:

- it is publicly accessible;
- licensing or reuse conditions are clear enough for public benchmarking;
- the study structure is documented well enough to justify the benchmark role;
- the chosen subset size is practical for automated or semi-automated reruns;
- the benchmark question is explicit.

## Review checklist

- What workflow stage does this benchmark validate?
- Is the dataset representative of a real use case?
- Are there privacy or consent caveats that limit public examples?
- Is the benchmark expected to be stable across software revisions?
- Can failures be interpreted meaningfully?
