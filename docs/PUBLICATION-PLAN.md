# Publication Plan

## Objective

Publish ClawNeuro in stages that match software maturity rather than rushing to a flagship paper too early.

## Stage 1 — architecture + benchmark preprint

Evidence needed:

- architecture is stable;
- benchmark suite exists;
- early public results are reproducible;
- project positioning is clear.

Output:

- preprint or technical report;
- benchmark report bundle;
- public demo datasets and rerun instructions.

## Stage 2 — JOSS submission

Evidence needed:

- open-source licensed repository;
- documentation;
- tests;
- clear research application;
- public development process.

Output:

- JOSS paper package;
- tagged release;
- DOI-backed archival snapshot.

## Stage 3 — field-specific methods / toolbox paper

Target profile:

- enough adoption or external interest to justify a broader methods paper;
- benchmark evidence on public data;
- concrete use cases from real labs;
- mature reporting and provenance story.

Candidate venues:

- Imaging Neuroscience
- NeuroImage
- conference-facing dissemination such as OHBM tutorials, posters, or workshops

## Companion artifacts

Every publication stage should be paired with:

- tagged software release;
- benchmark results;
- tutorial notebooks or demos;
- reproducibility dossier;
- citation metadata.
